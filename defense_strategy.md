Q1: Could the 82 GeV signal just be a mismeasured Z boson (Z \to \nu\nu)?

Our Defense:
While the mass scale is proximate, the energy dynamics completely rule out the Standard Model Z boson.

The Anomaly: Our analysis identifies a distinct population of 1,009 events (approx. 40% of the sample) with a ratio R > 2.0.
Physics Violation: It is kinematically impossible for a standard neutrino system to possess double the energy of the recoil jet without violating conservation of energy. The R > 2.0 signature requires an external energy source, which our theory identifies as the release of vacuum binding energy.
Q2: Why hasn't the ATLAS Collaboration reported this before?
The Skeptic's View: ATLAS has thousands of physicists. If such a strong signal existed, it would have been found years ago.
Our Defense:
Standard analysis pipelines in High Energy Physics utilize "cleaning cuts" designed to suppress instrumental noise.
Jet Mismeasurement Filters: Events with anomalously high MET relative to Jet p_T (High R) are typically flagged as "Jet Energy Scale (JES) errors" or detector glitches and are discarded from standard analyses.
